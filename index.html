Privacy Policy — Kite Royale

Effective date: 12 August 2026

Kite Royale ("we", "us", or "the app"), developed by Aditya Dudhal, respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Data we collect and why

  • Advertising (Google AdMob). Kite Royale shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and general device information in order to serve and measure ads. Your IP address may be used to estimate your approximate location (roughly city or region level, never a precise GPS position) so that ads are relevant to your area. Our analytics and leaderboard providers derive the same coarse, country-or-city-level location in the same way.
  • Online game services (Unity Gaming Services). To provide online multiplayer, matchmaking, and leaderboards, the app creates an anonymous player identifier and transmits gameplay data such as match results, scores, and connection information. No email address, password, or account sign-in is required to play.
  • Player display name. The game gives you an automatically generated, kite-themed nickname on first launch, and you can change it in the Garage. In online multiplayer this nickname is sent to the other players in your match so that it can be shown in the player list and above your flyer. Please do not use your real name or any other personal information as your display name.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase). To understand how the game is played and where players run into trouble, the app records anonymous gameplay events — for example that a match started or finished, which arena was used, how long it lasted, and whether an item was bought with in-game coins. These are tied to an app-generated identifier, never to your name or email. You can switch this off at any time in the app's settings.
  • Crash and error reporting (Firebase Crashlytics). When the game crashes or hits an error, a report is sent to us containing the error message, the technical stack trace, your device model and operating-system version, and which screen you were on. A copy is also kept on your device.
  • Performance samples. The app records how smoothly each match ran (frame rate and a coarse device class) so we can fix slowdowns on specific devices. No personal data is attached.
  • Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging). If you leave notifications enabled, the app registers a device notification token so we can send occasional messages about new content and events. Turning notifications off in the app's settings stops this.
  • Remote configuration (Firebase Remote Config). The app downloads settings values so we can adjust game balance and advertising frequency without an update. This is a download only — no data about you is sent.

What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location. The only name the game handles is the display nickname you choose for yourself, described above. We do not sell your personal data.

Children

Kite Royale is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will remove it.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Unity Gaming Services — unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Your choices

Analytics collection can be turned off inside the app at any time. Notifications can be turned off in the app's settings or in your device's system settings. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier from your device's Google settings.

Data retention and how to request deletion

To request deletion of data associated with your device in Kite Royale (developed by Aditya Dudhal), follow these steps:

  1. Email adityadudhal97@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  2. So we can locate your data, include your device's Google Advertising ID (Android: Settings → Google → Ads), or simply state that you played Kite Royale on your device.
  3. We will process your request and confirm by email within 30 days.

Data that is deleted: any advertising/analytics identifiers and anonymous gameplay data (match results, scores, and your display nickname) linked to your anonymous player identifier that are held by us or forwarded to our service providers (Google AdMob, Unity Gaming Services).

Data that is kept: aggregated, anonymised statistics that can no longer be linked to your device may be retained for legal and operational purposes. Crash reports and analytics events held by Google Firebase are retained under Google's own retention policies and are deleted on request as described above. The on-device copy of the error log, and your on-device game progress, are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Retention period: we do not collect data that identifies you personally. Advertising identifiers, analytics events and crash reports are retained by Google AdMob, Google Firebase and Unity Gaming Services under their own policies; deletion requests are forwarded to them where applicable.

Security

Data transmitted by the app is encrypted in transit, and we rely on the industry-standard security measures provided by Google and Unity.
